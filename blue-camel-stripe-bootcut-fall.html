Blue, Camel & Cream Cozy Bootcut

A cozy fall stripe in cream, camel and dusty blue gets a more flattering, polished line from slim bootcut denim and camel Chelsea wedges. The brown hobo and slim belt warm up the palette, while blue aviators and nickel-free sapphire-blue earrings repeat the sweater’s cool tone without overdoing it.

What to Buy First

  1. Sweater — it defines the color story and cozy fall personality.
  2. Jeans — the slim bootcut silhouette keeps the outfit balanced and works with the wedge boots.
  3. Boots — the camel lug-wedge grounds the whole look and makes it feel intentional.
  4. Bag — warm brown ties the belt and boots together.
Styling Tip: Let the sweater sit naturally over the waistband or do only a tiny front tuck; a full tuck will fight the relaxed lantern-sleeve shape. Keep the tank invisible except for a very slight neckline peek.
