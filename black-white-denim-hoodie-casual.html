Black, White & Denim Weekend Ease

A clean white ribbed tank and fluid black pants make an easy base, while the hooded denim jacket adds just enough structure and warmth. Retro sneakers and a taupe crossbody keep it casual without looking sloppy.

What to Buy First

  1. Hooded denim jacket — the hybrid denim + sweatshirt construction defines the look.
  2. Black wide-leg pants — the relaxed silhouette makes the outfit feel modern and comfortable.
  3. Retro sneakers — the black/cream contrast connects the top and pants.
  4. White ribbed tank — inexpensive, but getting the fitted proportion right matters.
  5. Taupe crossbody — pulls the warm neutrals together without competing with the outfit.

Styling Tip

Keep the tank fitted and the jacket slightly relaxed, then let the pants skim rather than puddle over the sneakers. That fitted-on-top/relaxed-on-bottom balance is what keeps this from reading like lounge clothes.
