Taupe Daisy Denim & Indigo Tote

This is an easy polished-casual outfit built around a soft taupe floral-sleeve sweater and straight vintage-wash jeans. The cream belt and taupe booties keep the neutrals warm, while the indigo tote adds just enough color without making the outfit busy.

WHAT TO BUY FIRST
  1. Sweater — the floral sleeves are the personality of the outfit.
  2. Jeans — a clean straight leg keeps the proportions current and flattering.
  3. Boots — the pointed neutral bootie gives the outfit its polished finish.
  4. Tote — the muted blue is the intentional color contrast.
  5. Belt — useful finishing detail, but the easiest piece to substitute.
STYLING TIP

Do a small front tuck so the cream belt shows, then let the sweater relax over the waistband at the sides. Keep the jeans long enough to meet the boot shaft without bunching heavily.
