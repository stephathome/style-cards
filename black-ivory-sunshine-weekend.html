Black & Ivory Sunshine Weekend

A graphic black-and-ivory mini dress does the heavy lifting, while a slim black cardigan keeps the look polished without covering the print. The canvas tote warms up the palette, and the chunky black flatform sandals make it feel modern and walkable.

What to Buy First

  1. Dress — the wavy black-and-ivory print defines the outfit.
  2. Sandals — the black flatform with a light platform gives the look its modern finish.
  3. Cardigan — useful for temperature changes, but easy to substitute.
  4. Tote — adds the warm natural/tan contrast that keeps the outfit from feeling too stark.

Styling Tip

Keep every added layer simple because the dress print is already busy. Wear the cardigan open, let the tan tote handles supply the warm accent, and keep jewelry lightweight and graphic.
