Muted Sage & Neutral Rainy-Day Casual

A soft sage sweater, ankle-length straight jeans and olive Chelsea rain boots create an easy fall base that still feels pulled together. A mushroom suede hobo and green boho accents keep the palette warm, earthy and intentional.

SEASONFall · Winter · Transitional
OCCASIONEveryday · Weekend · Errands · Travel

What to Buy First

  1. Sweater — it defines the muted sage color story.
  2. Jeans — the high-rise ankle straight silhouette keeps the roomy top flattering.
  3. Boots — the olive/cream/tan sole makes the outfit feel purposely outdoorsy.
  4. Bag — the mushroom suede ties every neutral together.
Styling Tip
Give the sweater a small front tuck or side tuck and let the jeans show a little ankle above the Chelsea boots. Keep jewelry green-and-gold rather than adding another strong color.
