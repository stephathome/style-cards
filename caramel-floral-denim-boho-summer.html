Caramel Floral + Denim Boho Summer

Soft, easy and feminine without feeling fussy. The caramel floral skirt brings the movement, while the white tank and light denim jacket keep the outfit relaxed; warm tan accessories make the whole palette feel intentional.

What to Buy First

  1. Floral maxi skirt — It defines the color story and boho movement.
  2. Light-wash denim jacket — The cropped casual layer is what keeps the skirt from reading too dressy.
  3. Camel flat sandals — They repeat the skirt's warmth and visually finish the hemline.
  4. Crochet crossbody — Adds the natural woven texture that makes the styling feel complete.

Styling Tip

Tuck the white tank fully or do a clean front tuck, then wear the denim jacket open. Keep every accessory in the warm natural family—camel, raffia, bamboo, light gold—so the blue flowers and denim stay fresh instead of competing.
