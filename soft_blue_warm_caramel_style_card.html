OUTFIT INSPIRATION

Soft Blue + Warm Caramel

WHAT TO BUY FIRST

Start with the pieces that define it

  1. CardiganDefines the entire blue + caramel color story.
  2. PantsThe warm camel bottom is the foundation; getting the tone right matters more than the brand.
  3. Studded mulesThey add the polished, slightly unexpected detail that makes the outfit feel styled.
  4. BagThe embossed tobacco leather reinforces the warm accessories and adds texture.
  5. EarringsA finishing pop that repeats the soft blue without needing to be exact.
STYLING TIP
Keep the browns warm, not perfectly matched.

The pants, belt, shoes and bag should live in the same camel-to-cognac family, but slight variation makes the outfit look collected instead of overly coordinated.
