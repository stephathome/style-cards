OUTFIT INSPIRATION

Soft Blush & Gray Denim

A soft blush, gray and ivory sweater with washed-gray flare jeans feels feminine without being fussy. A blush hobo bag and pale neutral accessories keep the palette coordinated, while a pointed mule makes the denim look polished.

WHAT TO BUY FIRST

Build the look in this order

1. Sweater — sets the palette.
2. Jeans — the gray flare silhouette is defining.
3. Shoes — pointed neutral mules add polish.
4. Bag — the blush color makes the palette intentional.

Styling Tip

Keep the belt and shoes in the same light-neutral family so the sweater and blush bag carry the color.
