Style Visualization · final recommended outfit

Sunny Gray Rugby + Denim Weekend

A cheerful butter-yellow and gray rugby sweater gives this casual outfit its personality, while light denim and gray sneakers keep it easy to wear. Cool silver accessories sharpen the palette without making it fussy. I left out the oversized yellow hair-scarf bow so the outfit stays cleaner and more wearable.

What to Buy First

  1. Rugby polo sweater — this is the outfit-defining piece.
  2. Light barrel jeans — the relaxed curved silhouette makes the outfit current.
  3. Gray sneakers — they keep the palette cohesive and practical.
  4. Gray phone crossbody — useful and visually ties the gray tones together.
  5. Silver belt — gives the denim a finished, intentional look.

Styling Tip

Use a loose front tuck so the belt shows, then push the sweater sleeves slightly up the forearm. Keep the accessories cool silver/gray and let the yellow stripe be the only strong color statement.
