Olive Stripes & Cream Denim Fall

A soft olive striped cardigan and warm cream square-neck tank give classic medium-wash denim a polished fall feel. The final version keeps the original earthy palette but swaps the oversized bow mule for a cleaner pointed-toe camel mule, so the outfit feels less fussy and more wearable.

WHAT TO BUY FIRST
  1. Cardigan — the olive stripe pattern defines the outfit.
  2. Jeans — the high-rise wide-leg silhouette sets the proportions.
  3. Shoes — a clean camel pointed mule gives the look polish without the oversized bow.
  4. Bag — the soft green hobo makes the palette feel deliberate.
  5. Tank — important for the warm cream contrast, but easy to substitute.
STYLING TIP

Keep the cardigan open enough to show the square neckline, then add the belt only if the jeans sit cleanly at the natural waist. The point is relaxed polish—not stacking every accessory at full volume.
