Gray + Olive Fall Casual

A relaxed gray-and-denim base gets a polished fall update from olive green accessories. The green retro sneakers and textured tote do most of the style work, while the jewelry repeats the color without making the outfit feel overdone.

What to Buy First

  1. Green retro sneakers — they make the gray-and-denim outfit look intentional instead of basic.
  2. Gray short-sleeve sweater — this is the clean neutral anchor.
  3. Medium-wash straight jeans — the relaxed silhouette keeps the outfit current.
  4. Olive textured tote — it repeats the shoe color and finishes the palette.
  5. Green earrings — the best finishing accent if you want the full look.
Styling Tip: Keep the green pieces in slightly different olive/forest shades. Matching them perfectly can look overly coordinated; related greens look more natural and expensive.
