Navy, Green & Rust Plaid Fall Polish

The plaid blazer does the heavy lifting here: navy keeps it grounded, green makes it fresh, and rust gives you an easy bridge to caramel accessories. Dark denim and a fitted white tee keep the outfit clean enough for lunch or errands while the heels and compact green bag make it feel intentionally styled.

What to Buy First

  1. Plaid blazer — it defines the outfit and color story.
  2. Dark button-front jeans — the clean dark wash and silhouette make the blazer look polished.
  3. Caramel Mary Jane heels — they pull the warm rust tones out of the plaid.
  4. Green shoulder bag — reinforces the blazer's green without looking overly matched.

Styling Tip

Keep the tee fully or mostly tucked so the slim caramel belt is visible. That gives the waist definition the wide-straight jeans need and keeps the blazer from feeling boxy.
