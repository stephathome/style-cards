Black + Ivory Stripe Mary Jane Polish

A crisp black-and-ivory outfit that feels polished without becoming dressy. The striped knit gives the look personality, while cropped black denim and mesh Mary Janes keep it modern and wearable. The soft ivory bag breaks up the black and makes the whole combination look intentional.

SEASONSpring · Fall · Mild Winter
OCCASIONEveryday · Lunch · Shopping · Coffee Date · Casual Office

What to Buy First

  1. Striped sweater vest — this is the visual signature of the outfit.
  2. Black cropped wide-leg jeans — the controlled cropped silhouette keeps the look current without going baggy.
  3. Mesh Mary Jane flats — they add the feminine, polished finish.
  4. Black cardigan — useful for layering and makes the outfit office-friendly.
  5. Ivory crescent bag — a finishing piece that softens the black-and-white palette.
Styling tip: Keep the cardigan open so the stripe pattern stays visible, and let the jeans hit above the ankle so the Mary Jane strap reads clearly.
Jewelry note: I intentionally left the screenshot earrings out of the final outfit because I could not independently verify that the hook/post, backing, and other ear-contact components are nickel-free. The outfit is complete without them.
