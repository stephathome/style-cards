Scarlet Varsity Denim Gameday

A polished game-day outfit built around a scarlet varsity sweatshirt, clean straight-leg denim and low-profile red sneakers. The cream-and-red cap and clear stadium bag make it practical for a tailgate or stadium, while brown sunglasses keep the accessories warm and grounded.

What to Buy First

  1. Varsity sweatshirt — it defines the entire look.
  2. Red retro sneakers — they repeat the sweatshirt color and make the outfit feel intentional.
  3. Straight-leg jeans — the cleaner silhouette balances the sporty top.
  4. Two-tone game-day cap — gives the outfit its unmistakable stadium feel.
  5. Clear stadium bag — practical and visually ties in through warm brown trim.
Styling tip: Keep the white tee just barely visible at the neckline or hem. That small hit of white connects the sleeve stripes, laces and hat without making the outfit look over-layered.
