Olive Tennis + Denim Weekend

A sporty olive matching set gets a casual, pulled-together finish from a light denim hoodie jacket and retro New Balance sneakers. The camo tote repeats the earthy palette, while simple studs and warm aviators keep the accessories polished without making the outfit fussy.

What to Buy First

  1. Olive skirt set — This is the outfit's defining color and silhouette.
  2. Light denim hoodie jacket — It creates the casual layered contrast that makes the look feel intentional.
  3. Olive New Balance 327s — They repeat the green without looking overly matched.
  4. Camo tote — Strong finishing piece, but a forest/olive tote can substitute.

Styling Tip

Wear the jacket open and let the olive set stay visually continuous underneath. That keeps the short skirt silhouette lengthened, and the chunky 327s look deliberate rather than heavy.
