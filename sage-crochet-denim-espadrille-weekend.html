Sage Crochet & Denim Weekend

This look mixes airy sage crochet with relaxed light-wash denim, then grounds it with warm brown espadrilles and earthy woven accessories. The result is casual and comfortable without looking unfinished—especially when the top stays loose and the cami underneath stays fitted.

WHAT TO BUY FIRST
  1. Sage crochet top — it defines the color and texture.
  2. Drawstring jeans — the relaxed rope-waist silhouette makes the outfit distinctive.
  3. Brown espadrilles — they add the warm grounding color the outfit needs.
  4. Olive woven bag — it repeats the sage family and finishes the boho-casual feel.
STYLING TIP

Keep the proportions intentional: loose crochet top + fitted cami + relaxed jeans. Let the brown sandals be the main warm accent, then repeat that warmth once more in the earrings or bag trim—no need to pile on extra jewelry.
