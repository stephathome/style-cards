Outfit Inspiration

Boho Floral Charcoal Weekend

A soft ivory botanical tee keeps this casual, while charcoal wide-leg pants make it look more intentional than jeans-and-a-tee. Light-wash denim and warm natural accessories add texture without making the outfit fussy.

What to Buy First

  1. Graphic tee — the botanical print establishes the whole palette.
  2. Charcoal wide-leg pants — the clean silhouette is what makes the look feel styled.
  3. Light-wash denim jacket — adds the casual layer and color contrast.
  4. Natural wedge sandals — finish the proportion and pull in the warm neutrals.
  5. Crossbody bag — worth adding if you want the full textured, tonal effect.

Styling Tip

Do a small front tuck on the tee and keep the jacket open. That little bit of waist definition prevents the relaxed tee + wide-leg pant combination from looking boxy.
