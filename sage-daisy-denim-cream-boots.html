Outfit Inspiration

Sage Daisy Denim & Cream Boots

Soft sage florals keep this outfit feminine without getting fussy, while the straight vintage-wash jeans make it easy to wear. Cream boots, a stone hobo, and a slim belt pull the neutrals together; the jewelry recommendations have been adjusted toward explicitly nickel-free materials.

What to Buy First

  1. Sage daisy sweater — it defines the entire look.
  2. Straight vintage-wash jeans — the silhouette keeps the sweater from feeling bulky.
  3. Cream V-cut boots — these are what make the outfit feel polished instead of basic.
  4. Stone hobo bag — ties the light footwear and belt together.
  5. Nickel-conscious jewelry — finishing detail, not the place to compromise on skin-contact materials.

Styling Tip

Keep the sweater slightly relaxed and let the jeans fall cleanly over the boot shaft. Use the slim beige belt only if you do a partial front tuck; otherwise let the sweater hang naturally and keep the accessories light.
