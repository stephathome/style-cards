STYLE VISUALIZATION

Classic Red, Navy & Denim Wedges

This is a cheerful, classic striped look grounded by cropped denim and warm neutral accessories. The cropped wide-leg jeans keep the silhouette current without letting the hem get overly baggy, while the espadrille wedge and soft cream hobo make it feel polished but still easy.

What to Buy First

  1. Striped sweater — It defines the entire outfit.
  2. Cropped wide-leg jeans — The controlled ankle-length silhouette is the key proportion.
  3. Neutral espadrille wedges — They turn basic denim into a finished summer look.
  4. Cream hobo bag — Pulls the light tones together and adds softness.
  5. Woven belt — A nice finishing texture, but not essential if the jeans fit cleanly without it.

Styling Tip

Use a small front tuck so the V-neck knit does not compete with the wide-leg jean. Let the ankle show clearly above the wedge strap; that keeps the proportions crisp on a 5'4" frame.

Jewelry note: I intentionally left the pictured Kendra Scott earrings out of the final outfit. The current Sophia/Sophee versions I found use plated brass or plated metal, and I did not find explicit confirmation that all ear-contact components are nickel-free.
