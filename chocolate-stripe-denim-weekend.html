OUTFIT INSPIRATION

Chocolate Stripe + Denim Weekend

Chocolate brown does the heavy lifting here: the striped tank, relaxed brown denim, sneakers, bag and cap all echo one another while the blue denim jacket breaks up the palette. The result is casual and sporty without looking sloppy, with just enough gold jewelry to polish it up.

What to Buy First

  1. Brown wide-leg jeans — the chocolate silhouette is the main thing that makes this outfit feel current.
  2. Striped tank — it ties the brown and ivory palette together and gives the denim jacket something graphic to frame.
  3. Retro brown sneakers — the low-profile gum-sole shape keeps the wide legs from feeling heavy.
  4. Denim jacket — important for the blue/brown contrast, but an existing medium-wash jacket can work perfectly.
  5. Brown crossbody — finishes the color story; the exact Michael Kors bag is optional.

Styling Tip

Let the brown jeans puddle only slightly over the sneakers. If the hem completely hides the shoe, the outfit loses the crisp retro-sneaker detail that keeps all the brown from looking too heavy.
