Outfit Inspiration

Soft Sage & Raffia Weekend

Soft sage, cream and sand keep this outfit quiet and polished while the light cropped denim keeps it relaxed. Natural woven accessories repeat the warm texture without making the outfit look overly beachy, and the green accents tie everything together.

What to Buy First

1
Color-block top
The vertical sage, cream and sand blocks define the whole palette.
2
Cropped light-wash jeans
The high-rise ankle-length silhouette keeps the look modern instead of sloppy.
3
Ivory raffia wedge slides
They add polish and repeat the natural texture without introducing a new color.
4
Natural structured bag
This is the best finishing piece once the core outfit is right.
Styling Tip
Let the jeans hit above the ankle and keep the top lightly tucked or just skimming the waistband. That small bit of waist definition keeps the wide leg, woven belt and platform wedge from feeling bulky.
