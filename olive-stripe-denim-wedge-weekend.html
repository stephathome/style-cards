Olive Stripe + Denim Weekend

This is a relaxed, put-together olive-and-denim outfit with enough shape to keep it from feeling sloppy. The high-neck stripe, cropped jacket and warm cork wedge give the wide-leg pants structure, while the denim bag ties the blue layer back into the look. The original earrings were replaced with a verified nickel-free silver option.

What to Buy First

  1. 1
    Striped tank — The olive-and-cream stripe is the clearest visual signature.
  2. 2
    Olive wide-leg pants — The silhouette and color carry most of the outfit.
  3. 3
    Denim jacket — The cropped proportion makes the wider pant shape work.
  4. 4
    Tan cork wedges — They warm up the palette and polish the casual pieces.
  5. 5
    Denim bag — Nice finishing piece, but not worth paying full price unless you love it.
Styling Tip: Keep the tank fitted and either choose ankle-length pants or show a little of the wedge. That bit of visible shoe keeps the wide-leg pants from visually shortening the outfit.
