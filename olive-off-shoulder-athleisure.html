Olive Off-Shoulder Athleisure

Cool, comfortable and a little edgy: a slouchy olive sweatshirt softens sleek black joggers, while light denim and retro sneakers keep the outfit casual instead of gym-only. The camo cap and quilted black bag add sporty texture without making the look feel busy.

What to Buy First

  1. Olive off-shoulder sweatshirt — it defines the color and relaxed neckline that make the outfit recognizable.
  2. Black tapered joggers — the clean, slim silhouette keeps the oversized top looking intentional.
  3. Neutral Nike Waffle Debut sneakers — the cream/black retro runner makes the whole look feel styled rather than basic.
  4. Light hooded denim jacket — adds the layered sporty texture for cooler weather.
  5. Black puffer crescent bag — reinforces the black accents and finishes the athleisure feel.
Styling Tip: Keep the sweatshirt loose and slightly asymmetrical, but keep the pants tapered and the bag compact. That contrast is what stops the outfit from looking oversized all over.
