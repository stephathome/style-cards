Black & Cream Stripe Midi Polish

The bold black-and-cream midi skirt does the heavy lifting, while the fitted black tank and cardigan keep the top half sleek. Natural wood jewelry and a cream/tan bag warm up the graphic palette, and simple black Mary Janes finish it without competing with the skirt.

Season: Fall · Spring · Mild Winter Occasion: Everyday · Lunch · Shopping · Coffee Date · Casual Workday

2

3

It keeps the proportions tidy and gives you the same layered finish.
4
Cream/tan shoulder bag
This is the best piece for softening the black and tying into the cream stripe.

Styling Tip

Keep the tank tucked and let the cardigan end close to the waist/upper hip. That preserves the skirt's long A-line shape; an oversized cardigan would hide the strongest part of the outfit.
