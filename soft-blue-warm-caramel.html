OUTFIT

Soft Blue + Warm Caramel

A polished-casual mix of powder blue, warm camel and cognac with crisp white underneath. The cardigan is the visual anchor; the brown leather accessories repeat the warmth and keep the blue from feeling too sweet.

What to Buy First

  1. Cardigan — it defines the color story and makes the outfit recognizable.
  2. Camel cropped pants — the warm color and clean cropped silhouette balance the sweater.
  3. Brown mules — they make the look feel intentional instead of like basic separates.
  4. Leather crossbody — the tobacco leather repeats the shoes and belt while adding texture.
Styling Tip: Keep all of the browns warm—camel, cognac and tobacco—rather than mixing in cool taupe or gray-brown. That warmth is what makes the powder blue look especially good.
