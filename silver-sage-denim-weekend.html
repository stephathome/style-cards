OUTFIT INSPIRATION

Silver Sage Denim Weekend

Soft gray, warm beige and medium-wash denim make this casual outfit feel pulled together without trying too hard. Metallic sneaker details and silver hoops sharpen the look, while the tote and khaki cap keep it relaxed enough for errands, travel or a weekend outing.

WHAT TO BUY FIRST
  1. Gray bomber — it defines the soft sporty layer.
  2. Cargo jeans — the relaxed wide-leg silhouette makes the outfit current.
  3. Silver-accent sneakers — they connect the gray jacket to the silver jewelry.
  4. Beige tote — it pulls the cap and warm neutrals together.
STYLING TIP

Keep the tank fitted and the jacket slightly relaxed so the wide-leg jeans do not overwhelm the outfit. Let the silver sneaker panels and hoops be the only shiny accents.
