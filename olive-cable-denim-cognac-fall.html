Olive Cable & Cognac Fall

Warm olive knit, straight dark denim, cognac ankle boots and a woven chocolate bag make an easy fall outfit that feels polished without being dressy. Nickel-conscious jewelry substitutes keep the olive-and-gold finish while respecting your jewelry requirements.

What to Buy First

  1. Olive cable-knit sweater — defines the look.
  2. Straight dark-wash jeans — keeps the silhouette balanced.
  3. Cognac ankle boots — creates the warm fall contrast.
  4. Woven brown hobo bag — finishes the color story.
STYLING TIP
Keep the sweater relaxed but the jeans straight rather than wide. A small front tuck keeps the outfit from feeling heavy, while cognac accessories warm up the olive and denim.
