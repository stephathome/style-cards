Outfit Inspiration

Black & White Floral Denim Wedges

A black-and-white floral sundress gets an easy, casual edge from a tied black denim shirt. Raffia wedges and a woven bag add summer texture, while black sunglasses and tassel earrings keep the palette crisp and coordinated.

What to Buy First

  1. Floral dress — the print and silhouette define the outfit.
  2. Black denim shirt — turns the dress from simply feminine into casual-polished.
  3. Espadrille wedges — create the summery, dressed-up-but-not-dressy finish.
  4. Black woven bag — reinforces the texture and color story.

Styling Tip

Keep every supporting piece solid black or natural raffia. The floral print already does the visual work, so extra color or busy jewelry would dilute the clean black-and-white effect.
