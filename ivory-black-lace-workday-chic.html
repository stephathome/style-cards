Ivory & Black Lace Workday Chic

A crisp ivory blouse and black wide-leg trousers make the foundation elegant without feeling stuffy. Tweed pumps, a slim white belt, clear frames and a structured black-and-white tote add graphic polish, while the jewelry is updated to verified nickel-free options.

What to Buy First

  1. Blouse — the lace cuffs define the look.
  2. Trousers — the clean wide-leg silhouette makes it polished.
  3. Tweed pumps — they supply the texture and pattern.
  4. Structured tote — pulls the black-and-ivory palette together.
  5. White belt — useful finishing contrast, but not essential if the blouse stays untucked.

Styling Tip

Tuck the blouse cleanly and let the lace cuffs show fully. Keep every other element crisp and restrained—the woven pumps already provide enough pattern, so the jewelry should stay delicate.
