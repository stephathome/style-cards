OUTFIT INSPIRATION

Teal Floral + Denim Boho

This is a strong late-summer-to-early-fall outfit: the floral maxi does the visual work, while denim makes it casual and the rust-orange accessories pull the print together. I kept the overall look, but swapped the jewelry recommendations toward options that can be explicitly verified nickel-free.

Late SummerEarly FallEveryday CasualWeekend OutingLunchTravel

Outfit at a Glance

1. Floral maxi dress
2. Denim tie-front shirt
3. Platform slides
4. Hobo bag
5. Orange hoop earrings
6. Gold bangle stack
7. Nail color

What to Buy First

  1. Floral maxi dress — defines the entire palette and silhouette.
  2. Denim tie-front shirt — turns a summery dress into an easy transitional outfit.
  3. Orange hobo bag — makes the rust/coral flowers look intentional.
  4. Neutral platform slides — keeps the outfit grounded without adding another color.
  5. Nickel-free orange hoops — a finishing pop, but not necessary if the bag already gives you enough orange.

Styling Tip

Tie the denim shirt at the narrowest comfortable point and leave a little dress bodice visible above the knot. Keep the jewelry simple because the dress, bag and shoe platform already give the outfit plenty of personality.
