STYLE VISUALIZATION

Rust + Gray Colorblock Denim Weekend

A cozy cable-knit colorblock cardigan does the heavy lifting here: rust gives it warmth, charcoal grounds it, and tan softens the transition into nude boots. A fitted white tee and medium-wash skinny jeans keep the proportions clean, while the charcoal hobo and translucent readers finish the look without competing with the knit.

WHAT TO BUY FIRST
  1. Cardigan — the rust/gray/tan colorblocking defines the look.
  2. Jeans — the close, medium-wash silhouette keeps the long cardigan balanced.
  3. Boots — the light neutral heel makes the outfit look intentional instead of heavy.
  4. Bag — charcoal repeats the cardigan's gray without adding another color.
STYLING TIP

Keep the tee fitted and let the cardigan hang open or button just one or two center buttons. The long knit already adds volume, so a slim jean and clean ankle boot keep the outfit polished.
