STYLE VISUALIZATION

Scarlet Eyelet & Caramel Wedges

A bright scarlet eyelet blouse makes this outfit feminine without feeling fussy. Cropped wide-leg denim keeps it relaxed, while caramel wedges, belt, and bag warm everything up. I swapped the pictured earrings for an explicitly nickel-free red hoop so the finishing detail works with your jewelry requirement.

WHAT TO BUY FIRST
  1. Red eyelet top — the piece that defines the look.
  2. Cropped wide-leg jeans — the silhouette matters more than the brand.
  3. Caramel wedges — they create the polished spring/summer finish.
  4. Brown belt — visually ties the shoes to the bag.
  5. Tan bag — useful, but a similar structured bag works perfectly.
STYLING TIP

Do a clean front tuck and let the belt show. Keep the jewelry simple—red hoops plus the blouse's ruffles are enough—so the warm caramel accessories can do the rest of the balancing.
