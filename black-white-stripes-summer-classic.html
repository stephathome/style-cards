Black & White Stripes, Summer Classic

A crisp striped tiered dress does nearly all the work, while a cropped black cardigan and structured black bag keep it polished instead of beachy. Black-and-white accessories make the look feel intentional without adding another color story.

What to Buy First

  1. Striped dress — It defines the entire look.
  2. Short black cardigan — The cropped proportion keeps the dress flattering and polished.
  3. Structured black bag — It keeps the outfit from reading too casual.
  4. Statement black-and-white jewelry — Add only after the core pieces are right.
Styling Tip: Keep every extra piece graphic and simple. The mixed stripe directions already create plenty of movement, so avoid adding another busy print.
