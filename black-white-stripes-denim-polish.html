OUTFIT INSPIRATION

Black & White Stripes, Denim Polish

A graphic off-the-shoulder striped sweater makes classic medium-wash denim feel intentional rather than basic. Black flats, belt, bag and geometric earrings repeat the stripe color and give the outfit a polished, pulled-together finish.

What to Buy First

  1. Striped sweater — It defines the outfit immediately.
  2. Wide-leg jeans — The relaxed silhouette is the second-biggest visual cue.
  3. Sleek black flats — They make the wide denim look polished instead of sloppy.
  4. Black bag — It anchors the black accessories and can be substituted easily.

Styling Tip

Let the sweater be the statement: keep the tank fitted, the accessories black, and the denim clean. If the sweater slips low on one shoulder, the visible black tank strap makes it look intentional.
