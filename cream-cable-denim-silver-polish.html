Cream Cable & Silver Denim Polish

A cozy cream cable knit and cropped blue ankle flares make the base feel classic, while bone sock booties and silver accents keep it polished. I kept the standout studded Kate Spade bag, but replaced the unverified inspiration earrings with a specifically nickel-free sterling-silver option.

What to Buy First

  1. Cable sweater — the creamy texture defines the whole look.
  2. Ankle-flare jeans — getting the cropped, fitted silhouette right matters more than the exact wash.
  3. Bone sock booties — they make the winter-white palette look intentional instead of matchy.
  4. White/silver belt — inexpensive, but it visually links the cream top to the silver hardware.
  5. Studded cream bag — the exact Kate Spade version is the splurge; a similar cream crossbody is completely workable.
Styling tip: Do a small front tuck so the white/silver belt shows, and let the cropped flare stop just above the boot shaft. That little break in the line is what keeps the outfit looking polished rather than bulky.
