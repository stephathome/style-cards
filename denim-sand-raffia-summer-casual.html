Outfit Inspiration

Denim, Sand & Raffia Summer Casual

A feminine denim jacket and fitted cream tank keep the top half defined, while sand-colored wide-leg pants make the outfit relaxed and current. Black sandals and sunglasses sharpen the soft neutrals, and the striped tote plus rattan earrings bring in warm natural texture.

What to Buy First

  1. Denim jacket — The fitted peplum shape defines the outfit.
  2. Sand wide-leg pants — The light color and relaxed silhouette create the soft neutral base.
  3. Black platform sandals — They keep the long pants balanced and add a crisp black anchor.
  4. Striped tote — It ties the cream, black and warm brown tones together.
  5. Square-neck tank — Important for the clean neckline, but easy to substitute.
Styling Tip: Keep the tank fitted and the jacket open or lightly buttoned at the waist. That gives the wide-leg pants room to flow without letting the whole outfit turn oversized.
